Stage: Onsite. 1:1 and small-group realtime chat across devices. Preview clarifying Q&A pins scope: groups capped ~250 members (personal communication, not broadcast - keeps synchronous-path fan-out manageable); new group members can read FULL prior history by default (membership semantics = full shared history); durable ordered conversation history is the single source of truth, while presence/read-receipts/notifications are fast lossy layers around it.
