> DrustZ 整理的补充研究笔记。公司与轮次信息沿用作者的来源记录。

资料范围：公开预览与可核实要点。

Stages: Screening + Onsite. Massive integer dataset partitioned across k workers (0..k-1), each worker only sees its own unsorted slice. Part 1 (q ...76a): distributed MODE (most frequent integer, ties -> smallest) such that no worker ever receives all raw data and network usage is minimized. Part 2 (q ...805): distributed MEDIAN under the same constraints, handling odd/even sizes. Site follow-ups: worker crashes/network timeouts; hash skew mitigation for hot values; extend to top-K frequent; async messaging effects on synchronization/termination; complexity vs sort-and-merge; k-th smallest generalization; skewed data distribution.
