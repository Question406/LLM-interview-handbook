          <div class="followups"><div class="mini-title">常见追问与答案</div><details class="followup"><summary>0% pass-rate 永远没用吗？</summary><div class="follow-answer"><p>对纯 outcome group-relative gradient，当次 batch 基本没直接信号；但可以用 SFT/teacher distillation、增加 exploration、process reward 或稍后 policy 变强后重新纳入。</p></div></details><details class="followup"><summary>100% pass-rate 能做什么？</summary><div class="follow-answer"><p>可用于行为保持、效率优化、短答偏好、regression eval；只是对同题 reward-relative learning 信号弱。</p></div></details><details class="followup"><summary>如何动态重估 difficulty？</summary><div class="follow-answer"><p>周期性用 current policy 小样本 rollout，维护 moving pass-rate / uncertainty；按 skill/domain 分桶，避免频繁全量评估。</p></div></details></div>
